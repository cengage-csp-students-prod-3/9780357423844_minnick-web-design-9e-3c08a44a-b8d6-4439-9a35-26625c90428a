Guideline 1.1 emphasizes providing text alternatives for non-text content, such as images, videos, and interactive controls, to ensure that users who cannot see or interact with visual content can still understand the information. Guideline 1.2 focuses on offering alternatives for time-based media, including captions for videos and audio descriptions, so that users with hearing or vision impairments can access the content. Guideline 1.3 encourages creating adaptable content that can be presented in different ways without losing meaning, such as presenting content in a logical sequence or providing proper headings. Guideline 1.4 highlights making web content easy to see and hear, which includes using sufficient color contrast, allowing text resizing, and ensuring proper spacing for readability.
