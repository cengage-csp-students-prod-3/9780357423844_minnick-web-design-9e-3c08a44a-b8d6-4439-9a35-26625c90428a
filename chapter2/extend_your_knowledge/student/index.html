Web Accessibility Principles

▪ Web accessibility ensures all users can access content. ▪ It covers various disabilities and enhances usability.

▪ Perceivable content is available to all senses. ▪ Operable interfaces can be used via different devices.

▪ Understandable means content is clear and logical. ▪ Robust sites adapt to future technologies.
