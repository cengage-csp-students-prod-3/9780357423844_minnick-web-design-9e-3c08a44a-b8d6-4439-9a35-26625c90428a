About Us

The Wildlife Rescue Center is dedicated to the care, rehabilitation, and release of injured and orphaned wild animals. Our mission is to ensure the survival of native species while educating the community about wildlife conservation.

Rescue Stories

Every year, we rescue and care for hundreds of animals, including birds, mammals, and reptiles. Our trained staff and volunteers work tirelessly to help them recover and return to their natural habitats.
